Experience commanding capability and refined comfort with the 2023 GMC Sierra 1500 Denali Crew Cab Diesel 4x4, designed for those who demand power and luxury in every drive.

This robust truck features a turbocharged straight 6-cylinder diesel engine paired with a 10-speed automatic transmission with electronic precision shift, delivering impressive performance and efficiency. The 4-wheel drive system, combined with a locking limited-slip differential, ensures confident handling on all terrains. Tow hooks and a tow hitch enhance its utility for heavy-duty tasks.

Step inside the spacious crew cab with seating for five, including heated and cooled front bucket seats with power adjustments and lumbar support for both driver and passenger. Seat memory and mirror memory add convenience, while the rear bench seat offers a pass-through for added versatility. The heated rear seats provide comfort for all passengers.

Safety is paramount with features such as automatic high beams, blind spot monitoring, cross-traffic alert, front collision mitigation and warning, lane departure warning, lane keeping assist, and a comprehensive airbag system. Stability and traction control, along with 4-wheel disc brakes and tire pressure monitoring, further enhance security.

Exterior amenities include LED headlights, fog lamps, running boards, integrated tailgate step, and heated, power-folding mirrors. The advanced aerial view display system, back-up camera, rear parking aid, and remote engine start combine technology with practicality, making every journey safer and more enjoyable.
