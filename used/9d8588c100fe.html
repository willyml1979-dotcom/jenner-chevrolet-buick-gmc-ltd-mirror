Experience the 2023 Kia Telluride X-Line: a sophisticated AWD SUV with advanced safety, premium comfort, and versatile seating for seven in sleek gray.

This 2023 Kia Telluride X-Line offers a powerful 3.8L V6 engine paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission featuring Drive Mode Select, delivering responsive and smooth performance on any terrain. Its all-wheel-drive system and brake-actuated limited-slip differential enhance stability and traction, ideal for Canadian roads year-round. The exterior is finished in a refined gray with aluminum wheels, fog lamps, LED headlights, and a rear spoiler, complemented by a generic sun/moonroof to bring natural light into the spacious cabin.

Inside, black leather seats provide heated and cooled comfort for both front and rear passengers, with power adjustments and memory settings for the driver and front passenger. The third-row bucket seats ensure ample space for seven occupants, while rear bucket seats and rear A/C add to passenger convenience. The leather steering wheel is heated and equipped with audio controls for ease of use.

Safety is paramount with features like blind spot monitoring, front collision mitigation, lane keeping assist, rear collision mitigation, and an aerial view display system. Additional amenities include adaptive cruise control, a heads-up display, hands-free power liftgate, remote engine start, and a premium sound system, making every drive secure and enjoyable.
