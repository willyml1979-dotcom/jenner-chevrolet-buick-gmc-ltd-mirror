Experience the 2024 Buick Envista Sport Touring in striking Copper Ice Metallic, blending style, technology, and safety in a compact SUV designed for confident urban driving.

Powered by a turbocharged 1.2L ECOTEC 3-cylinder engine delivering 137 hp and 162 lb-ft of torque, this front-wheel-drive Envista offers a smooth 6-speed automatic transmission for efficient and responsive performance. Its Sport Touring Preferred Equipment Group includes premium synthetic bucket seats with driver-adjustable lumbar support, heated front seats, and a power driver seat, all finished in Ebony with Santorini Blue accents for a refined interior atmosphere.

Stay connected and comfortable with features such as wireless charging, a power liftgate, and Rainsense front wipers included in the Convenience II Package. The cabin is equipped with climate control, rear A/C, Bluetooth connectivity, satellite radio, and a WiFi hotspot, ensuring convenience on every journey.

Safety is paramount with automatic headlights, high beams, blind spot monitoring, cross-traffic alert, lane departure warning, lane keeping assist, and front collision mitigation. Additional protection comes from multiple airbags, stability and traction control, and a rearview camera.

Finished with aluminum wheels and performance tires, this Envista combines sporty handling with practicality, making it an ideal choice for drivers seeking a versatile and well-equipped compact SUV.
