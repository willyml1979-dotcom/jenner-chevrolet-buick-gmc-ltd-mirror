Experience the 2024 Chevrolet Equinox EV LT in Iridescent Pearl Tricoat, combining advanced electric performance with cutting-edge safety and comfort features.

This all-electric SUV is powered by a smooth electric motor paired with a Nickel Cobalt Manganese traction battery, delivering efficient and responsive driving without the need for a traditional transmission. Its 1-speed automatic transmission ensures seamless acceleration, while front and rear performance tires provide confident handling on the road. The aluminum wheels complement the sporty exterior, finished in a striking Iridescent Pearl Tricoat.

Inside, the Equinox EV LT offers premium synthetic bucket seats with heated front seating and driver-adjustable lumbar support, ensuring comfort on every journey. The rear bench seat includes a pass-through for added versatility. The black interior is enhanced by a heated steering wheel and an adjustable steering column for a personalized driving position.

Safety is paramount with features such as Blind Spot Monitor, Lane Departure Warning and Lane Keeping Assist, Front and Rear Collision Mitigation, Cross-Traffic Alert, and Automatic Highbeams. Convenience is elevated with a hands-free power liftgate, remote engine start, back-up camera, rear parking aid, and an aerial view display system. Bluetooth connectivity, satellite radio, WiFi hotspot, and smart device integration keep you connected on the go.
