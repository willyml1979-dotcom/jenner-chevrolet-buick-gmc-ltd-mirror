Experience the 2020 Honda HR-V Touring in Platinum White Pearl, combining sophisticated style with advanced safety and comfort features for your everyday adventures.

This AWD small SUV is powered by a responsive 4-cylinder engine paired with a Continuously Variable Transmission (CVT) featuring an Econ Mode button and dual shift mode, delivering smooth and efficient performance. The Touring trim offers leather seats with heated front seats, ensuring comfort during colder Canadian months. The black interior complements the premium leather steering wheel, adding a refined touch to your driving environment.

Safety is paramount with features such as Front Collision Mitigation, Lane Departure Warning, Lane Keeping Assist, and Brake Assist, alongside multiple airbags including front, side, and rear head airbags. Automatic headlights and high beams, fog lamps, and LED headlights enhance visibility in all conditions. The vehicle also includes stability and traction control, ABS with 4-wheel disc brakes, and a tire pressure monitor for added confidence on the road.

Convenience is enhanced with keyless entry and start, an auto-dimming rearview mirror, rain-sensing wipers, and a back-up camera. Enjoy adaptive cruise control and steering assist for effortless highway driving. The sun/moonroof invites natural light into the cabin, while aluminum wheels and a rear spoiler complete the sporty exterior look. This HR-V Touring is ready to meet your driving needs with style and capability.
