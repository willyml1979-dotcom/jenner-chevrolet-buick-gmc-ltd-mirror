Experience the 2018 Chevrolet Equinox Premier True North Edition, a diesel-powered AWD SUV designed for confident driving in all conditions with premium comfort and advanced safety features.

This Summit White Equinox combines a turbocharged 1.6L DOHC 4-cylinder diesel engine delivering 137 horsepower and 240 lb-ft of torque with a smooth 6-speed automatic transmission featuring driver shift control. The all-wheel-drive system ensures enhanced traction and stability on diverse terrains.

Inside, enjoy luxurious Jet Black leather seats with heated front and rear seating, power-adjustable driver seat with lumbar support, and seat memory for personalized comfort. The cabin is equipped with a Chevrolet MyLink 8-inch colour touchscreen featuring navigation, Bluetooth streaming audio, and an intuitive infotainment system. Multi-zone climate control maintains ideal temperatures for all passengers.

Safety is paramount with features like blind spot monitoring, cross-traffic alert, automatic headlights, stability and traction control, multiple airbags, and child safety locks. The Confidence & Convenience II Package adds driver assistance technologies for peace of mind.

Additional highlights include a power sunroof, hands-free power liftgate, remote engine start, fog lamps, aluminum wheels with wheel locks, and a roof rack with cross rails for extra cargo versatility. The Equinox Premier True North Edition offers a refined, capable driving experience tailored for Canadian roads and lifestyles.
