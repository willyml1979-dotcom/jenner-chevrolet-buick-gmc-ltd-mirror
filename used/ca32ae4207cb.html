Experience the 2020 Chevrolet Bolt EV LT in striking Cajun Red Tintcoat, combining advanced electric performance with thoughtful comfort and safety features.

This all-electric small wagon offers a powerful 200 horsepower electric motor paired with a 1-speed automatic transmission and front-wheel drive, delivering smooth acceleration and efficient driving. Equipped with a lithium-ion traction battery and plug-in electric fast charge capability, it’s designed for convenience and eco-friendly travel. The Bolt EV LT features cloth bucket seats with heated front seats and a pass-through rear bench, comfortably seating five passengers in a stylish grey interior.

Safety is comprehensive with multiple airbags including front, side, knee, and rear head airbags, plus stability and traction control systems, ABS with 4-wheel disc brakes, and child safety locks. Automatic headlights, daytime running lights, and HID headlights enhance visibility, while heated mirrors and a heated steering wheel add comfort in colder climates.

Technology and convenience blend seamlessly with keyless entry and start, remote engine start, power liftgate, back-up camera, Bluetooth connectivity, satellite radio, WiFi hotspot, and smart device integration. Aluminum wheels with performance tires front and rear complete the package, ensuring this Bolt EV LT is ready for urban adventures and beyond.
