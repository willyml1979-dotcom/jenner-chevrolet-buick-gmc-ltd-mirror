Experience the 2023 Chevrolet Bolt EUV Premier: a stylish electric SUV with advanced safety, premium comfort, and cutting-edge technology for the modern Canadian driver.

This Silver Flare Metallic Bolt EUV Premier offers a powerful 200 hp electric motor with 266 lb-ft of torque, delivering smooth front-wheel drive performance. Equipped with a lithium-ion traction battery and plug-in electric fast charge capability, it’s designed for efficient, eco-friendly driving. The Premier trim features a Jet Black leather-appointed interior with heated and cooled front seats, heated rear seats, and a power driver seat with adjustable lumbar support for exceptional comfort.

Safety is paramount with automatic headlights, high beams, blind spot monitoring, cross-traffic alert, lane keeping assist, and front collision mitigation. Additional airbags, stability and traction control, and rear parking aids provide peace of mind on every journey. The Sun & Sound Package enhances your drive with a dual-pane panoramic sunroof, a 10.2-inch HD touchscreen with Chevrolet Infotainment 3 Plus, connected navigation, and a Bose premium 7-speaker audio system.

The Redline Edition adds sporty 17-inch gloss black aluminum wheels with red accents and matching exterior details. Practical features include a power liftgate, keyless entry and start, rain-sensing wipers, and an aerial view display system. Enjoy three years of remote access for convenient vehicle control via your smartphone. The Bolt EUV Premier combines innovation, style, and comfort for your daily adventures.
