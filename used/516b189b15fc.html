Power, precision, and premium comfort combine in the 2023 Chevrolet Silverado 3500HD High Country, crafted for demanding Canadian drivers who require unmatched capability.

This diesel-powered powerhouse boasts a Duramax 6.6L Turbo-Diesel V8 engine delivering 445 horsepower and an impressive 910 lb-ft of torque, ideal for towing heavy loads or hauling gear through rugged terrain. Equipped with a 10-speed Allison automatic transmission and a locking limited-slip differential, it ensures smooth, confident control whether on highways or off-road trails. The Z71 Off-Road Package enhances durability with Rancho-tuned shocks, skid plates, and hill descent control, perfect for navigating challenging landscapes.

The High Country trim elevates driver experience with heated and cooled leather-appointed bucket seats featuring adjustable lumbar support and memory settings for both driver and passenger. Power-retractable assist steps with LED lighting provide convenience and style, while the Multi-Flex tailgate offers six versatile load and access options, simplifying worksite tasks. Advanced safety features like Blind Spot Monitor, Cross-Traffic Alert, and an aerial view display system provide peace of mind during every journey.

Finished in striking Iridescent Pearl Tricoat with jet black leather interior, this crew cab 4x4 pickup blends rugged capability with refined luxury, tailored for those who demand excellence in every drive.
