2025 Chevrolet Express Cargo Van: Your Reliable Partner for Every Job, Every Day, in Every Way

Introducing the 2025 Chevrolet Express Cargo Van, designed for those who demand reliability and performance in their daily work. This full-size cargo van, finished in Summit White, is powered by a robust 4.3L V6 engine with Direct Injection and Variable Valve Timing, delivering 276 horsepower and 298 lb-ft of torque. Its 8-speed automatic transmission with overdrive and tow/haul mode ensures smooth handling and efficiency, making it perfect for navigating both city streets and highways.

Safety is paramount with features such as Stability Control, Traction Control, and a comprehensive airbag system, including Front Head and Side Air Bags. The Back-Up Camera and Tire Pressure Monitor further enhance your peace of mind on the road. Inside, the Med Dark Pewter interior offers comfort with high-back bucket seats and a convenient swing-out storage bin.

Stay connected with the Bluetooth Phone Connection Kit, allowing hands-free calls and music streaming. The Remote Keyless Entry with panic button offers added convenience, while the Driver Convenience Package, featuring Tilt-Wheel and cruise control, makes long drives more comfortable. The 2025 Chevrolet Express Cargo Van is your dependable partner, ready to tackle any task with ease and efficiency.
