Discover the 2026 Chevrolet Trax 2RS: a stylish, turbocharged compact SUV designed for urban explorers seeking advanced safety, comfort, and connectivity in every drive.

The Trax 2RS combines a spirited 1.2L turbocharged 3-cylinder engine with a smooth 6-speed automatic transmission, delivering 137 horsepower and 162 lb-ft of torque—ideal for navigating city streets and highways with confidence. Front-wheel drive enhances fuel efficiency without compromising agility, while performance tires and aluminum wheels provide a sporty stance and responsive handling.

Step inside to find premium synthetic bucket seats with striking jet black and red accents, featuring heated front seats and a power driver’s seat for personalized comfort. The thoughtfully designed cabin includes a pass-through rear seat and cargo shade, perfect for weekend adventures or daily errands.

Safety is paramount with features like Blind Spot Monitor, Cross-Traffic Alert, Front Collision Mitigation, Lane Keeping Assist, and Adaptive Cruise Control, helping you stay alert and protected. The rear parking aid and back-up camera simplify tight parking situations, while keyless entry and remote engine start add convenience.

Stay connected on the go with Bluetooth integration, steering wheel audio controls, WiFi hotspot capability, and smart device compatibility, ensuring your drive is as enjoyable as it is efficient. The Trax 2RS is ready to complement your active lifestyle with style and substance.
