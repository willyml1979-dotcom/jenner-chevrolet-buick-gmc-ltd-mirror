Experience the 2027 Chevrolet Bolt LT: a stylish, efficient electric SUV designed for the urban Canadian driver seeking advanced safety, comfort, and cutting-edge technology.

The Bolt LT in Summit White combines eco-conscious driving with practicality, offering a smooth front-wheel-drive electric motor paired with a 1-speed automatic transmission. Designed for convenience, it features keyless entry and start, heated front seats and steering wheel, and a power-adjustable driver’s seat, ensuring comfort on every journey. Stay connected effortlessly through Bluetooth, WiFi hotspot, and satellite radio, while the back-up camera and rear parking aid make city manoeuvres stress-free.

Safety is paramount with an extensive suite of driver-assist technologies including adaptive cruise control, blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, and front and rear collision mitigation. Automatic headlights and high beams enhance visibility in all conditions, while cross-traffic alert and evasion assist add extra confidence when navigating busy streets or parking lots.

The Bolt’s aluminum wheels and all-season tires provide reliable traction year-round, and the included Level 2 and fast charging adapters ensure quick, convenient recharging at home or on the go. With its spacious interior featuring cloth bucket seats and a pass-through rear bench, the Chevrolet Bolt LT is the smart, stylish choice for Canadians embracing electric driving.
