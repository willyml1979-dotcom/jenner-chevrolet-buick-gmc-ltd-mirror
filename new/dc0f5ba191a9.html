Command the road with power and precision in the 2026 GMC Sierra 1500 Elevation, a truck that redefines capability and style.

The 2026 GMC Sierra 1500 Elevation in Titanium Rush Metallic is designed for those who demand more from their truck. With a robust 5.3L EcoTec3 V8 engine delivering 355 hp and 383 lb-ft of torque, this truck is ready to tackle any challenge. The 10-speed automatic transmission with tow/haul mode ensures smooth handling, whether you're cruising the highway or navigating rugged terrain. The Max Trailering Package enhances your towing capacity, making it perfect for hauling heavy loads with ease.
