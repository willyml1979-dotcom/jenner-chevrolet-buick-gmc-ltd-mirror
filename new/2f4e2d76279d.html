Experience the perfect blend of efficiency, technology, and safety in the 2027 Chevrolet Bolt LT, designed for the modern Canadian driver seeking an eco-friendly and connected ride.

The Chevrolet Bolt LT in Mosaic Black Metallic offers a forward-thinking electric motor paired with front-wheel drive, delivering smooth, responsive acceleration without the noise or emissions of a traditional engine. Its Level 2 and Fast Charging Adapter Package ensures you spend less time plugged in and more time on the road, ideal for busy urban lifestyles or weekend getaways.

Inside, the Jet Black interior features heated front bucket seats and a power-adjustable driver seat, providing comfort during chilly Canadian winters. Stay connected effortlessly with built-in Bluetooth, WiFi hotspot capability, and satellite radio, all accessible via an intuitive infotainment system. The auto-dimming rearview mirror and heated mirrors add convenience and safety, especially in low-visibility conditions.

Advanced safety technologies like Blind Spot Monitor, Lane Keeping Assist, Front and Rear Collision Mitigation, and Cross-Traffic Alert work together to provide peace of mind on every drive. Adaptive Cruise Control and Remote Engine Start further enhance your driving experience, making daily commutes and longer trips more enjoyable and stress-free.

With its sleek design, cutting-edge features, and commitment to sustainability, the Chevrolet Bolt LT is the smart choice for Canadians ready to embrace the future of driving.
