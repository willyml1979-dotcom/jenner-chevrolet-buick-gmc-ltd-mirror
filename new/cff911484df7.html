Built for the toughest Canadian jobs, the 2026 Chevrolet Silverado 3500HD CC Work Truck combines rugged durability with advanced towing and off-road capabilities to keep you productive in any condition.

Powered by a robust Duramax 6.6L Turbo-Diesel V8 engine delivering 470 horsepower and an impressive 975 lb-ft of torque, this heavy-duty crew cab chassis cab is engineered to handle demanding workloads with ease. The 10-speed automatic transmission and dual rear wheels provide smooth power delivery and enhanced stability, while the 4x4 drivetrain with electronic Autotrac and locking limited-slip differential ensures confident traction on challenging terrain. Designed with the professional in mind, the Silverado 3500HD features skid plates protecting critical components like the oil pan and transfer case, and tow hooks for quick recovery. The WT Fleet Convenience Package adds heated, power-adjustable vertical trailering mirrors for optimal visibility when hauling large loads. Inside, durable vinyl seats and a pass-through rear bench offer practicality and comfort during long workdays. The upfitter switch kit with multiple configurable circuits allows seamless integration of aftermarket electrical accessories, tailored to your specific job needs. Safety remains paramount with front collision mitigation, automatic high beams, and a rear camera kit for precise manoeuvring. Finished in Summit White with steel wheels and all-terrain tyres, this truck is ready to tackle any project from coast to coast.
